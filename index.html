Find the gap between your games and the pros'.

Enter your Steam ID. Pro Gap reads your recent ranked games and lines them up against pro players on the same hero and position: last hits, deaths, item and level timings, fights and wards.

Options

No public match data?

Make your games visible. In Dota 2, go to Settings, Options, Social and turn on Expose Public Match Data. It applies to games played after you switch it on.

Nothing is stored on a server. Your browser talks straight to OpenDota and keeps a cache, so a second visit loads almost instantly.
